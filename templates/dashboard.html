The Night at La Collina

The evening before Integration Day, Stou and four other members of the board gathered at La Collina to make the final preparations for the following morning.

It wasn't the smoothest meeting. There had been a few disagreements, and not everyone seemed to be on good terms. Still, they had things to finish, and the evening went on as planned.

Eventually, Mahmoud, Amine, Emna and Omar left La Collina, while Stou decided to stay a little longer.

He still had a few things to take care of before the big day. The activities needed some final adjustments, the materials had to be checked, and there were still arrangements to make sure everything would run smoothly.

He told the others he would finish the preparations and lock up once he was done.

The following morning, the caretaker arrived at La Collina and discovered that Stou was dead in the study.

The preparations for Integration Day had been left unfinished. What was supposed to be an exciting day had suddenly turned into something nobody expected.

The police were contacted, but until they arrived, many questions remained unanswered.

What happened that night? Who was involved? And what was everyone hiding?
